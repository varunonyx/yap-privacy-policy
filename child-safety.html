Child Safety Standards

App Name: Yap
Last Updated: October 19, 2026

Zero-Tolerance Policy: Yap has a strict, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). We are committed to maintaining a safe environment and strictly prohibit any content or behavior that exploits or endangers minors.

1. Prohibited Conduct

Users of Yap are strictly prohibited from:

  • Creating, sharing, soliciting, or promoting CSAM in any form.
  • Engaging in any form of grooming, sextortion, or sexual exploitation of minors.
  • Using the app to facilitate the trafficking, abuse, or exploitation of minors.

2. Reporting and Moderation

Yap provides users with immediate tools to report safety concerns. Because Yap is an anonymous voice network, we rely on user reports combined with immediate technical enforcement:

  • In-App Reporting: Users can tap the "Report" button on the active call screen to flag inappropriate behavior instantly.
  • Force Mute & Disconnect: Reporting a user instantly cuts their microphone audio and terminates the call.
  • Permanent Device Bans: Devices used to violate these standards are permanently blocked from the platform using hardware fingerprinting (ANDROID_ID).

3. Law Enforcement Cooperation

Yap complies with all relevant child safety laws. We will cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies and report any confirmed CSAM or CSAE activity to the appropriate regional and national authorities (such as NCMEC or local Indian authorities).

4. Designated Point of Contact

In accordance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy, Yap has designated a point of contact who is prepared to speak about our CSAM prevention practices and compliance.
