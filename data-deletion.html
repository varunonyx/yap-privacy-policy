Data Deletion Request

App Name: Yap

Developer: Yap Telecom Inc.

At Yap, we respect your privacy and give you control over your data. This page explains how you can request the deletion of specific data associated with your Yap account without deleting your entire account.

What data can be deleted without deleting your account?
  • Call Logs & Match History: The record of who you matched with, the duration of the call, and timestamps.
  • Chat Messages: Ephemeral text messages sent during calls (note: these are already automatically deleted when the call ends).

How to Request Data Deletion

You can delete your call logs and match history directly inside the Yap app by following these steps:

  1. Open the Yap app and ensure you are logged in.
  2. From the main Lobby screen, tap the three-dot menu in the top right corner.
  3. Tap on Law & Vulnerability Audit Centre.
  4. Scroll down and tap "Purge Call Metadata" (or "Delete Call Logs").
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted. Your call history will be immediately and permanently erased.

Alternatively, you can clear your call history from the main Lobby screen by tapping the trash can icon next to your history.

What data is deleted and what is kept?

Data Deleted Immediately: Your call logs, match history, and timestamps are permanently removed from our active databases.

Data Kept (and Retention Period):

  • Account Information (Email): Kept until you request full account deletion via the "My Data" screen.
  • Device Identifier (ANDROID_ID): Kept indefinitely only if your device has been flagged or banned for violating community safety guidelines (e.g., harassment). If you are not banned, this data is not linked to your active profile.

How to Delete Your Entire Account

If you wish to delete your entire account (including your email address and profile), you can do so directly in the app:

  1. Open the Yap app.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu and select My Data.
  3. Tap "Delete My Account" and confirm.

Contact Us

If you have trouble using the in-app tools, you can request data deletion by emailing our support team:

Email: dpo@yapapp.com

Please include the email address associated with your Yap account in your request. We will process your request within 7 business days.
