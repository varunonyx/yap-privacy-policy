Privacy Policy for Yap

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

Welcome to Yap ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, anonymous environment for voice communication. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using the Yap application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

The Core Promise of Yap: Yap is built for anonymity. We do not require your real name, and we never record or store your voice calls. Voice data is processed in real-time purely to facilitate the live connection between you and your matched stranger.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Email Address: We collect your email address to create and manage your account, and to send you necessary verification and security emails.
  • Pseudonym: You are assigned a random anonymous name (e.g., "Silent Owl #203"). You may edit this name at any time.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Voice Data (Real-time processing only): When you are on a call, your microphone captures your voice. This audio is transmitted directly to your matched peer using WebRTC (encrypted P2P or via secure TURN servers). We do not record, store, or listen to your audio calls. Once the call ends, the audio is permanently gone.
  • Device Identifiers (ANDROID_ID): We collect and cryptographically hash your device's hardware identifier. This is used strictly for community safety, fraud prevention, and enforcing permanent bans on devices that violate our terms.
  • App Activity (Call Logs): We store metadata about your calls (duration, timestamp, and the anonymous pseudonym of your partner) locally on your device and in our secure database to provide the app's functionality. This data is automatically and permanently deleted after 90 days.

C. Chat Messages

  • Ephemeral Text Chat: If you use the in-call text chat, the messages are encrypted locally on your device (AES-256) before being sent. These messages are automatically deleted when the call ends.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the app's core matchmaking and voice-calling features.
  • To verify your email and secure your account.
  • To enforce our Community Guidelines, including the "Force Mute" feature and permanent device bans.
  • To calculate and display your "Karma" score, which helps improve matchmaking quality.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers necessary to run the app:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure Authentication, Firestore Database (storing your email and anonymous metadata), and Crashlytics (to report app crashes).
  • TURN Servers (Metered/Twilio): Used temporarily when a direct peer-to-peer connection cannot be established, ensuring your call stays connected. These servers route encrypted audio and do not record it.

4. Data Security

We take security seriously. Your data is encrypted in transit using industry standards (TLS 1.3, DTLS-SRTP for voice). We utilize strict Firebase Security Rules to ensure users cannot access data belonging to others.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Voice Data: Not stored. Destroyed after the call.
  • Chat Messages: Automatically deleted at the end of each call.
  • Call Logs: Automatically purged from our systems after 90 days.
  • Account Data (Email): Retained until you delete your account.

Account Deletion: You can delete your account at any time directly within the app by navigating to Menu > My Data > Delete My Account. This will permanently erase your email, profile, and call history from our active databases.

6. Children's Privacy

Yap is strictly intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we discover an account belongs to a minor, it will be immediately terminated.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location (including under India's DPDP Act or the GDPR for EU users), you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can exercise these rights through the "My Data" section of the app or by contacting us.
